# Campaign plan

The campaign has 100 stages: ten zones of ten contiguous stages, all playable.
Zone k holds stages 10k+1 to 10k+10, and its tenth stage is the zone boss.

| Zone | Stages | Setting | Boss stage · boss |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| King's Road (`city`) | 1–10 | Outer fields, market wards and bell-tower approaches | Bell Tower Gate · Grave Lord |
| Saltwake Docks (`harbor`) | 11–20 | Tide-broken quays, chain cranes and wreck piers | Leviathan Wake · Harrow Tidemaster |
| Emberforge March (`foundry`) | 21–30 | Coal spurs, smelter rows and furnace crowns | Furnace Crown · Iron Warden |
| Ashen Ward (`quarantine`) | 31–40 | Purge cloisters, leech tents and sealed vault checkpoints | Pale Crown · Plague Monarch |
| Thornwall Pass (`thornwall`) | 41–50 | Cliff roads, avalanche shrines and watch forts | Thornwall Gate · Thornwall Chieftain |
| Hollow Basilica (`basilica`) | 51–60 | Ruined cathedrals, ossuary courts and reliquary vaults | Reliquary Cataclysm · Reliquary Tyrant |
| Mire of Saints (`mire`) | 61–70 | Bog causeways, drowned chapels and plague ferries | Mire Bell · Mire Behemoth |
| Sunfall Steppe (`steppe`) | 71–80 | Burned waystations, open grassland forts and roaming siege camps | Sunfall Warcamp · Steppe Warlord |
| Gloamwood Verge (`gloamwood`) | 81–90 | Thorn groves, witch circles and haunted timber roads | Gloamwood Heart · Gloamwood Witch |
| Crownfall Citadel (`citadel`) | 91–100 | Bridge forts, breach yards and the inner keep | Ashen Throne · Ashen Regent |

Four zones also field a mid-zone boss in slot 5: Tidecaller (stage 15), Plague
Archon (35, returning at 37), Bone Pontiff (55) and Dread Sovereign (95).

Zone titles and summaries live in `scripts/core/RouteCatalog.cs`. Each zone's
stage target and clear reward (gold, food and a relic) live in
`scripts/core/CampaignPlanCatalog.cs`. Late-campaign systems switch on at zone
boundaries (stages 31, 51, 61 and 81); the thresholds are in
`scripts/core/CampaignPacing.cs`.

## Data and saves

- `data/stages.json` is generated by `scripts/analysis/expand_campaign.py` from
  the 60-stage source `scripts/analysis/legacy/stages-60.json`. Each zone keeps
  its six authored stages and gains four derived ones; difficulty is re-spread
  over 1–100, and unit and spell `UnlockStage` values are rewritten. Change the
  source or generator and regenerate with `--write` rather than editing
  `data/stages.json` by hand.
- `scripts/core/CampaignRenumbering.cs` moves saves older than version 46 from
  the 60-stage numbering onto the current stages. Its table mirrors the
  mapping the generator prints, so keep the two in sync.
