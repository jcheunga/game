# Crownroad — 10 district concept sheets

New visual ideas for **CROWNROAD: SIEGE OF ASH**, generated on **6 October 2026** with the **built-in image_gen tool**.

The ten sheets collectively explore all **52 named roster entries** in `data/units.json` (19 allies, 19 regular enemies and 14 bosses), the ten campaign districts, wagon skins and weapon mounts, fortifications, modular environment props, projectiles, spell effects, relics and rewards. District assignments are brainstorming groupings; they do not change the game's roster or gameplay.

Each board combines a readable character lineup, isolated asset concepts, a small environment strip and material/palette direction. The common identity is road-worn royal miniatures: oak, blue-black iron, brass, teal cloth and amber lanterns for the Lantern Caravan; bone, weathered iron, ruined crimson heraldry and restrained supernatural light for the Rotbound Host.

These are idea boards, not packed animation atlases or production-ready transparent sprites. The King's Road board carries the game title in its header. No generated art has been wired into the running game.

## Sources

- `THEME_BIBLE.md`: medieval siege fiction, factions and route identity.
- `CAMPAIGN_PLAN.md`: ten district names and settings.
- `data/units.json`: current unit names and IDs.
- `art/blender/ART_DIRECTION.md` and `art/remaster/rk/palette.py`: materials, faction palette and lighting.
- Existing Shield Knight, Iron Warden and Lantern Caravan images were visually inspected for context. Generation used written briefs rather than editing those assets.

## Prompts

`prompts.json` records the full final prompt for every generated sheet, including the common visual brief and district-specific subjects. The PNGs are saved here independently of the built-in tool's original generated files.
