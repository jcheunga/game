Four original Crownroad instrumental loops generated for this project by `scripts/tools/create_audio.py`:

- title: 16 bars, 84 BPM; lute, strings, flute and bells
- campaign: 16 bars, 92 BPM; exploring arrangement
- shop: 16 bars, 88 BPM; quieter lute and strings
- battle: 16 bars, 116 BPM; strings, flute, drums and brush percussion

No third-party recordings or samples are used. Sound effects in `assets/sfx` are synthesized from the same source. Circular room reflections keep the music loops seamless. Godot enables looping at playback; effects remain one-shots.
