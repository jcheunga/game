# Handoff: work left alone by the royal UI conversion (2026-10-06)

The royal UI conversion (commit `fc604eb`, see `docs/ROYAL_UI.md`) replaced the screens, battle
backdrops, campaign maps and item pictures with painted art, then removed the art and code they
replaced. Some leftovers were deliberately **not** touched because they belong to other work: unit,
caravan and enemy art (planned for a separate thread), combat effects, and music. This file lists
them with the evidence found, so a fresh session can pick them up.

## Before you start

- Build: `export PATH="/usr/local/share/dotnet:$PATH"; dotnet build` (Godot is `godot` on the PATH).
- Run review drivers one at a time, each with a **unique** save suffix in the required family, for example
  `godot --path . res://scenes/tests/UiReviewSmoke.tscn -- --save-suffix=ui-review-$(date +%s)`.
  The default `UiReviewSmoke` flow does not reset progress, so reusing a suffix carries the last run's
  save into the next one (an upgraded unit, spent gold) and fails for no real reason. Avoid `$RANDOM`
  inside a zsh loop: it gave the same value twice.
  Families: `ui-review-*`, `mobile-review-*`, `combat-review-*`, `world-art-review-*`,
  `blender-review-*`, `exploration-review-*`, `feedback-review-*`, `healthbar-review-*`,
  `privacy-review-*`, `motion-review-*`.
- `./scripts/verify_all.sh` builds, validates data (prints asset coverage) and runs the server tests;
  it runs no Godot drivers.
- "Push" means commit and push to `main` directly.
- Some test helpers read private members by name through reflection, so the compiler doesn't catch
  a rename or removal; grep `scripts/tests` for the old name.

## 1. Battle visual effects (decided 2026-10-08)

The user asked to drop the circles on routine events: every damage number, enemy spawn, unit death, deploy (the
door burst and the deploy-momentum ring), base hit and wagon weapon shot. Those `SpawnEffect` calls are gone; the
events keep their particles, numbers and death dissolve. Ability, spell, splash, boss and enemy-special circles
stay, since they show what something does. The inventory below is the original survey.

Almost every growing circle comes from `BattleEffect` (`scripts/combat/BattleEffect.cs`). It grows a
filled disc or 3 px ring from a start to an end radius while fading. Two helpers create it:
`BattleController.SpawnEffect` (about line 5534; it squashes the circle onto the ground plane, ×0.42
vertically) and `ProjectileEffects.Ring`. The most frequent ones:

| Trigger | Where | Shape |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Every damage number | `SpawnDamageFeedback` (about line 5556) | ring 2→6–10 px, 0.11 s |
| Every enemy spawn at the gate | `SpawnEnemyUnit` | ring 10→26, 0.22 s |
| Every unit death | death flow (`deadUnit` effect) | filled disc 8→24, 0.22 s |
| Player deploy at the wagon door | `WagonDoorExit` effect | filled 6→18, 0.28 s |
| A unit hits a base | base-hit effect | filled 8→26, 0.18 s |
| Splash projectiles land | splash branch | ring 8→splash radius |
| Wagon weapon fires | `FireBaseWeapon` (`BattleController.BaseWeapons.cs`) | ring 3→15 |
| Active abilities (cleave, wall, charge, sweep, howl, inspire…) | `BattleController.cs` ability handlers | rings 8→36–64 |
| Enemy specials (rally, jam signal, raise fallen, burrow, siege deploy, death bursts) | special handlers | rings 12→54–88 |
| Boss phase warning (campaign bosses at 55% health or less) | `DrawBossPhaseWarnings` (`BattleController.BossPacing.cs`) | fixed faint disc and a sweeping orange arc, 1.6 s |
| Challenge ghost markers (replayed deployments in challenge mode) | `DrawChallengeGhostMarkers` | ring 18→32 |

Non-circle effects include weapon swing streaks, impact sparks, projectile trails, glows and impact
bursts (`ProjectileEffects.cs`, `ProjectileStyles.cs`), death dust and dissolve
(`BattleDeathEffects.cs`, `DeathPresentation.cs`), base debris and damage smoke, the red low-hull
vignette, and end-of-battle gold or smoke bursts. Spells have their own styled effects (`SpawnEffect`
with Fireburst, HealBloom, FrostBurst, LightningStrike or WardSigil styles). The ambient weather
particle system was removed in the cleanup; it had already been switched off and never ran.

## 2. Unit art (separate thread)

- **Visual-class sprite sheets:** `assets/units/<visual_class>.png` + `.json` (about 43 MB, 22
  classes) plus `assets/units/warband_sprites.png` (2.4 MB). They are fallbacks only:
  `UnitSpriteLoader` (`scripts/combat/UnitSpriteLoader.cs`) prefers the per-unit sheet, and all 52
  units have one.
  - **Exception:** `assets/units/shield.png` is used by the Stone Barricade spell (visual class
    "shield"). Keep it, or give the barricade its own sprite first.
  - `art/remaster/pack.py` (about line 183) copies per-unit sheets onto the class names; retire that
    together with the class sheets.
- **Procedural unit drawing:** `Unit.cs` still has procedural visuals, used only when a unit has no
  sheet. The draw path starts at about `Unit.cs:873`; per-class attack flashes are at about
  1008–1402, and the aura ring and buff halo at about 1428–1452. With every unit sheeted this never
  runs.
- **Codex legacy foes:** 17 codex entries use ids that don't match `data/units.json`, so the codex
  still shows old Blender portraits for them (`assets/ui/portraits/codex/`).
  - Entries: `enemy_heavy`, `enemy_exploder`, `enemy_ranged`, `enemy_rusher`, `enemy_buffer`,
    `enemy_hexer`, `enemy_tank`, and `boss_*` (10 raid bosses, also shown by `RaidMenu`).
  - They are aliases of current units. The old pack tool's table:
    - heavy→`enemy_brute`, exploder→`enemy_bloater`, ranged→`enemy_spitter`,
      rusher→`enemy_saboteur`, buffer→`enemy_howler`, hexer→`enemy_jammer`, tank→`enemy_crusher`
    - grave_lord→`enemy_boss`, tidecaller→`enemy_boss_docks`, iron_warden→`enemy_boss_forge`,
      plague_archon→`enemy_boss_ward`, thornwall→`enemy_boss_pass`,
      bone_pontiff→`enemy_boss_basilica`, mire_behemoth→`enemy_boss_mire`,
      steppe_warlord→`enemy_boss_steppe`, gloamwood_witch→`enemy_boss_verge`,
      dread_sovereign→`enemy_boss_citadel`
  - Mapping these to unit figures (`UnitFigure.For`) in `CodexMenu.Portrait` and `RaidMenu` would
    retire the last portraits. Check whether the codex also lists the current ids, which would make
    these entries duplicates.

## 3. Caravan and structure art (separate thread)

- **Fallbacks behind the battle-v2 art:** `assets/structures/war_wagon*.png` and `gatehouse.png`
  (about 11 MB), with `assets/structures/siege_atlas.png` (2.2 MB) behind those. The battle-v2 plates
  (`assets/structures/battle-v2/`) cover the default wagon, all 7 skins and the gatehouse.
  - The atlas is loaded by `BattlefieldTextureLoader.TryLoadStructure` (line 17).
  - The flat files are still checked by `AssetAuditService` ("Structures", "Caravan skins") and
    `art/blender/pack_assets.py` / `verify_assets.py`; update those if you retire them.
- **Weapon mounts:** `assets/structures/mount_{arrows,ballista,firepot}.png` are in use. The enemy
  stronghold's frost and hex mounts were removed along with its weapon.

## 4. Particles and projectiles

- `assets/particles/particle_trail.png` is not used by any effect; it is only listed in
  `AssetCoverageCatalog.ParticleTextureIds` and built by `art/remaster/build_particles.py`.
- `art/remaster/build_items.py` still renders spell and relic icons that the game no longer uses
  (spells and relics show painted pictures from `assets/ui/royal/items`). Its reward and meta icon
  outputs are still used. The other item builder, `art/blender/build_items.py`, was already limited
  to reward and meta icons and particles.

## 5. Music (resolved)

Resolved by the audio work (`66bde14`): battles call `MusicPlayer.PlayBattle` with their route, so each zone plays
its own track, boss fights switch to `battle_boss` / `battle_boss_final`, and `assets/music/README.md` lists all
19 loops.

## 6. Battle hints (resolved 2026-10-08)

First-time battle hints (`TryShowTutorialHint`) now appear one at a time in `BattleHintBanner` above the card dock,
for long enough to read, and a tap moves on. The status line (`SetStatus`) stays hidden: its messages are
running commentary, not hints.

## 7. Combat code that never ran (removed 2026-10-08)

Traced and deleted because it could never activate: campaign pressure echo, adaptive waves, commendation, stage
missions with their aftermath and counter surges, bonus objective pressure, field orders, the convoy command stub,
late conditions and boss pressure (`BattleController` lost about 1,900 lines), their `GameState` status builders, the
campaign objective overrides and adaptive-wave objective in `StageObjectives`, the "Boss command" lines in
`StageEncounterIntel`, and `BattleMutatorCatalog` with its save fields, the unreachable "Rule Breaker" achievement and
mutator hint. Still live: the route doctrine pulse, the boss phase, and challenge mutators (`AsyncChallengeCatalog`).

Left for a decision:

- **Stage hazards and mission events** are still in `data/stages.json` (88 stages with hazards, 23 with mission
  events), in the stage details text (`StageEncounterIntel`: "Hazards", "Mission events") and in two objective types
  (`hazard_hits_limit`, `mission_event_success`), but battles simulate neither, so those objectives always resolve
  against zero hits and zero missions. Either build them or remove the data, text and objective types together.
- **The legacy result card** (`_endPanel` in `BattleController.Ui.cs`) is still used for LAN race and online rooms.
