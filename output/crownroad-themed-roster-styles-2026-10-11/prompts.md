"Ashen Regent" — imposing adult-proportioned undead royal final boss in broad blackened armor with restrained aged brass edges, broken bone crown, flowing crimson mantle and two-handed greatsword. A distinctive noble commanding silhouette and subtle green eyes, more regal and threatening than common enemy infantry.
