Crownroad

The kingdom fell to the Rotbound Host. Its roads still stand. Escort the Lantern Caravan's war wagon from keep to keep and break every gatehouse between you and the Crownfall Citadel.

The siege

Wagon on the left. Gatehouse on the right.

Every battle is fought on one lane. Your war wagon holds the west end and a Rotbound gatehouse holds the east. The ground between changes hands one troop at a time. Lose the wagon and the stage is lost. Break the gate and the road opens.

  1. First

    Courage builds

    Courage fills on its own as the fight goes on, and every troop spends it. A War Hound costs 12 and a Ballista Crew costs 36, so each card is a choice. Spells draw on mana, which every kill earns.

  2. Then

    Deploy and cast

    Tap a troop card and the troop marches out of the war wagon's door to join the line. Drag a spell onto the field to cast it where the fight is thickest.

  3. Finally

    Break the gate

    Push your line up to the gatehouse and bring it down before the Host reaches your wagon. When mana is full, the wagon's lantern looses Arcane Barrage across the whole field.

The Lantern Caravan

Thirty-two troops and thirty spells

New troops join as the caravan pushes deeper. Peasants and Militia hold the first gates. Poleaxe Knights, Banner Knights and Ballista Crews only reach you near the end of the road.

The road

Ten regions, each held by a grave lord

Ways to play

Campaign, Endless March and challenges

A hundred stages

Campaign

Explore each region's map, defeat its nine leaders, then face the boss at its gate. Later stages add hazards, cursed ground and battlefield events to the fight.

Roguelite survival

Endless March

Survive waves that keep getting harder. Draft upgrades at each checkpoint, choose which fork of the road to take, and retreat in time to bank your spoils.

Async and LAN

Challenges

Share a seeded board with a code like CH-04-PRS-4821, take on the daily featured board, or host a LAN race and compare times on a shared scoreboard.
