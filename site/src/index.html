The siege

Wagon on the left. Gatehouse on the right.

Every battle is fought on one lane. Your war wagon holds the west end and a Rotbound gatehouse holds the east. The ground between changes hands one squad at a time. Lose the wagon and the stage is lost. Break the gate and the road opens.

  1. First

    Courage builds

    Courage fills on its own as the fight goes on. Every squad and rite spends it. A War Hound costs 10 and a Ballista Crew costs 52, so each card is a choice.

  2. Then

    Deploy and cast

    Send squads down the lane and cast rites where the fight is thickest. Hold the blue ring to earn forward deployments. Hold the gold ring to claim courage, repairs or siege supplies.

  3. Finally

    Break the gate

    Gate archers, ballistas and firepots fire back, and every shot is telegraphed 0.7 seconds ahead. Bring the gate down before the Host reaches your wagon.

Ways to play

Campaign, Endless March and challenges

Sixty stages

Campaign

Every stage has a forward post to capture and a supply cache to claim. Cursed stages split the field into two danger pockets with safe lanes between them.

Roguelite survival

Endless March

Survive waves that keep getting harder. Draft upgrades at each checkpoint, choose which fork of the road to take, and retreat in time to bank your spoils.

Async and LAN

Challenges

Share a seeded board with a code like CH-04-PRS-4821, take on the daily featured board, or host a LAN race and compare times on a shared scoreboard.
