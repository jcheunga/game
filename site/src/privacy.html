What we collect and why

This covers the Crownroad game, its online services, and this website.

DataWhat it's used forHow long we keep it
Player ID and sessions A random ID that the game creates the first time it runs. It looks like CVY- followed by letters and numbers, or ACC- for an account first created at sign-in. When the game can reach our server, it registers this ID with your nickname and our server issues a sign-in session, even if you play as a guest. The game sends your player ID with every request to our server. For each session we store a one-way hash of its token (never the token itself), and when it was created, last used, expires or was ended. We use them to run online features and keep your progress and purchases attached to you. The player ID is kept until you delete your account. A session stops working after 30 days, or earlier when you sign out, the game replaces it or you delete your account. Its record stays with your player ID until you delete your account. After a deletion we keep only a one-way hash of each session that was still active, for 31 days, so a repeated deletion request gets the same answer.
Sign-in accounts (optional) If you sign in, we link that account to your player ID so you can get your progress back on another device.
  • Email: your email address. We email you a six-digit code and keep only a one-way hash of it.
  • Google: Google tells us your Google account ID, your email address and whether it is verified. We check that it is verified, then keep only the account ID.
  • Sign in with Apple: we don't ask Apple for your name or email address. We keep the account ID Apple gives this game, and Apple's refresh token, encrypted, so that deleting your account can revoke Crownroad's access to your Apple account.
  • Google Play Games: your Play Games player ID. On Android, if your phone is signed in to Play Games, the game signs you in with it automatically when it connects to our server, unless that could replace progress on your device. Signing out in Account stops this.
  • Game Center: your Game Center player ID. We also keep a one-way hash of each Game Center sign-in signature so it can't be used twice.
Your device also keeps which method you signed in with and, for email, your address, so Account can show it. These stay on the device and aren't part of cloud saves. 		Linked accounts are kept until you delete your account. An unfinished sign-in, which holds the email address or account ID and a hash of the code, expires after 10 minutes and is cleared out as new sign-ins start. Game Center signature hashes expire after 20 minutes. If Apple can't be reached when you delete your account, the encrypted Apple token is kept only until Apple accepts the revocation, for up to about three weeks.
Nickname The name you choose in Settings, also called your callsign. It starts as "Lantern", or "Caravan" for an account first created at sign-in. Other players can see it in online room lists, room scoreboards and LAN races. Until you change it or delete your account. Online rooms you joined keep the nickname you used at the time.
Game results Challenge results (score, stars, time, wagon health and defeats), achievements and when you unlocked them, daily challenge scores and completion times, and the online rooms you hosted or joined with your result and place. Used for leaderboards, challenges and fair play. Challenge and daily leaderboards show your player ID with your score, and room scoreboards show your nickname and player ID. Anyone using the game can see them. Until you delete your account.
Live race updates While you race in an online room, your current time, wagon health and defeats are shared with the other racers. Deleted after 24 hours.
Cloud save Your progress, gameplay settings, nickname, player ID and the IDs of purchases already delivered. It uploads after each stage you win once the game is connected to our server, and when you choose Upload in Settings. Cloud saves exclude your sign-in session, which sign-in method and email address you use, server connection settings and your analytics and crash-report choices. Restoring progress keeps the current device's sign-in and privacy choices. When you sign in to an account that has a cloud save, the game downloads it and keeps a copy of your previous progress on your device. One save per player ID, replaced by each upload. Deleted with your account.
Purchases When you buy something through Google Play or the App Store, the game sends the store's purchase details to our server, which checks them with Google or Apple. We record the item, the store, the store's order or transaction ID, a one-way fingerprint of the store's purchase token, the time, what the purchase credited (gold, food or a unit unlock) and your balance and history of purchased currency. We use them to deliver what you paid for, stop one purchase being credited twice or claimed by another player, limit purchases to 10 an hour, handle refunds and answer questions about your purchases. Purchases on desktop through Stripe record Stripe's checkout session and payment IDs instead of a store fingerprint. We never receive your card or bank details. As long as tax and accounting law requires us to keep purchase records. If you delete your account, we delete your currency balance and history and keep only the purchase records, under a random code that isn't linked to you.
Refunds If Google or Apple refunds a purchase, our server learns of it: Apple sends it a notice, and it checks Google Play's list of refunded orders every hour. We may also record a refund made through Stripe or our support. We record the notice (the store, the order or transaction ID, the kind of notice and when it arrived), mark the purchase refunded, and take back what it credited. The next time the game connects, it removes that gold or food from your save once and tells our server how much it took back. Currency you have already spent isn't taken below zero; we record the difference. A unit unlocked by the Adventurer's Kit stays unlocked. If Apple reverses a refund, the currency is given back the same way. With the purchase record they belong to. If you delete your account, they stay with it under the same random code.
Gameplay analytics (only if you opt in) Events such as starting a session (with your highest stage and gold), and starting and finishing a stage (with the stage, difficulty, squad, result, stars, time and wagon health), sent with your player ID, game version and platform. Used to understand difficulty and improve balance. Off until you turn on "Share analytics" in Settings, and you can switch it off at any time. Deleted after 30 days, or sooner if you delete your account.
Crash reports (only if you opt in) If you turn on "Send crash reports" in Settings and the game hits an unexpected error, it sends the error type and message, the technical trace, the game version, the platform, the screen you were on and your player ID, so we can fix bugs. Off by default, separately from gameplay analytics, and you can switch it off at any time. Deleted after 90 days, or sooner if you delete your account.
Player reports If you report another player in an online room, we record both player IDs, the reason you chose and a note about the race. Deleted after 90 days. Reports you made and reports about you are deleted with your account.
Connection data and server logs Your IP address is used to connect you to our servers. To limit abuse, we briefly keep a one-way hash of your IP address or session token. Our web server's access logs for the game's online service record your IP address, the address requested (which can include your player ID), your player ID, the time, and app or browser details. The game server's own logs record your player ID, the address requested, the result and how long it took. The hashes expire after about a minute. Logs rotate automatically and are overwritten as they fill.

On your device

The game stores your save file (with a backup copy), a queue of results waiting to upload, a copy of your progress each time you sign in to a different account or sign out, and any screenshots you take, in the game's own storage on your device. The save file includes your sign-in session and which sign-in method you use, with your email address if you signed in with email. Deleting the game deletes them.

LAN races

LAN races connect devices on the same local network directly. The host's local IP address is shown on screen so others can join. Your nickname, chosen squads, ready state, live race updates and final result are shared with the other devices in the room. This traffic doesn't pass through our servers.

This website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking, and we don't keep visitor logs for it. Its fonts are served by Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google, which receives your IP address and browser details when the page loads. See Google's privacy policy.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal data or share it for advertising. We share it only as follows:

  • Other players see your nickname, player ID, scores and race results in online and LAN features.
  • Google confirms who you are when you sign in with Google or Google Play Games, and confirms purchases made through Google Play: our server sends Google the purchase token to check it, and asks Google Play which orders were refunded. Google Play links each purchase to a one-way fingerprint of your player ID, never the ID itself. Google also serves this website's fonts. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Apple confirms who you are when you use Sign in with Apple or Game Center, and confirms purchases made through the App Store: our server sends Apple the transaction ID to check it, and Apple tells our server when it refunds a purchase. When you delete your account, we send Apple your refresh token so it can revoke Crownroad's access. The App Store links each purchase to a one-way fingerprint of your player ID, never the ID itself. See Apple's privacy policy.
  • Our email delivery provider sends sign-in codes for us. It receives your email address and the code, and may only use them to deliver the email.
  • Stripe processes purchases made outside the mobile stores, such as on desktop, and receives your player ID so the purchase reaches the right profile. You enter payment details on Stripe's own page. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Hosting and database providers run the servers and database that store the data above for us, under contracts that only allow them to process it on our instructions.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or to protect players, the game or the public from fraud or harm.

Our providers may process data outside your country. When the law requires it, we use recognised safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

Your choices and rights

  • Analytics and crash reports: switch either option on or off in Settings. Turning analytics off also clears events waiting to be sent. These choices stay on your device when you restore a cloud save.
  • Nickname: change it in Settings at any time.
  • Sign-in: you can play as a guest without signing in. Signing out in Account ends the session, starts a new guest with a new player ID on that device, and stops automatic Google Play Games sign-in there. The account stays linked to your progress, so signing in again brings it back.
  • Delete your account: in the game, choose Settings, then Account, then Delete account. See what deleting removes and keeps.
  • Access, correction, copy, deletion and objection: depending on where you live, you can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, get a copy, delete it, or object to how we use it.

To make a request, email {{support_email}}. If you sign in with email, write from that address. Otherwise include your player ID: it's the code after "Profile:" under Records & replay on the multiplayer screen. Because guests have no password, we may ask for details only someone with your device would know before we act, so nobody else can delete or read your data. We reply within 30 days.

Resetting your campaign in Settings starts a new player ID on your device and signs you out there, but doesn't delete data linked to the old ID, including any sign-in accounts linked to it. To remove that, delete your account before you reset, or email us the old ID. See how to delete your data.

Deleting your account

Delete account in the game's Settings works for guests and signed-in players alike, and takes effect at once. It asks you to confirm first and can't be undone.

  • Deleted: your player ID, nickname, sessions, linked sign-ins (your email address, Apple, Google, Game Center and Google Play Games), cloud save, game results and leaderboard entries, achievements, daily completions, arena and raid records, friends and gifts, guild membership, online rooms you hosted and your seats in other rooms, player reports you made or that name you, your in-game currency balance and history, gameplay analytics and crash reports.
  • Sign in with Apple: we also ask Apple to revoke Crownroad's access to your Apple account. If Apple can't be reached, our server keeps trying for about three weeks.
  • Kept: the record of each purchase (the item, store and platform, the store's transaction ID, the receipt fingerprint, the time and the currency it delivered) and of any refund of it. Tax law requires it, and it lets us handle refunds and stops a purchase being delivered twice. We move it to a random code that isn't linked to you or to any player. If a store refunds a purchase after you delete your account, we mark the kept record refunded; there is nothing left to take back.
  • Shared things: a guild you led passes to its top contributor, and an empty one is removed. Other players' arena records keep their own result without your player ID.
  • Elsewhere: your device starts again as a new guest, and deleting the game removes what is left on it. Copies in our encrypted database backups are removed as those backups expire. Apple, Google and Stripe keep their own records of payments made through them.

If you can't open the game, email {{support_email}} with your player ID and we'll delete your account within 30 days. See how to delete your data.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to your local data protection authority. We'd appreciate the chance to fix it first.

Children

Crownroad isn't directed at children under 13, or the minimum age for consenting to online services in your country if that is higher. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children under that age. If you believe a child has used our online features, contact us and we will delete their data.

Security

Data moves between the game and our servers over encrypted HTTPS connections. Session tokens, sign-in codes and Google and Apple purchase tokens are stored only as one-way hashes, Sign in with Apple refresh tokens are encrypted, and access to our servers and database is restricted. No system is perfectly secure. If you find a vulnerability, please report it as described on the support page.
