What we collect and why

This covers the Crownroad game, its online services, and this website.

DataWhat it's used forHow long we keep it
Player ID and session A random ID (it looks like CVY- followed by letters and numbers) that the game creates the first time it runs, plus a sign-in session our server issues to that ID. We use them to run online features and keep your progress and purchases attached to you. We store only a one-way hash of the session token. The player ID is kept until you ask us to delete your data. Sessions expire after 30 days.
Callsign The name you choose in Settings (the default is "Lantern"). Other players can see it in online rooms, on shared scoreboards and in LAN races. Until you change it or ask us to delete your data.
Game results Challenge results, scores, achievements, daily completions, and which online rooms you joined and how you placed. Used for leaderboards, challenges and fair play. Until you ask us to delete your data.
Live race updates While you race in an online room, your current time, wagon health and defeats are shared with the other racers. Deleted after 24 hours.
Cloud save A copy of your save file (progress, settings, callsign and player ID) is uploaded after you win a stage, so your progress is backed up. Replaced by each new save. Kept until you ask us to delete your data.
Purchases The item you bought, the store and platform, the store's order or transaction ID, a one-way fingerprint of the store receipt, the time, and your in-game currency balance and history. Used to deliver what you paid for, handle refunds and prevent fraud. We never receive your card or bank details. As long as the law requires us to keep purchase and tax records. We keep only the minimum needed after a deletion request.
Gameplay analytics (only if you opt in) Events such as starting a session and finishing a stage, with your player ID, game version and platform. Used to understand difficulty and improve balance. Off until you agree, and you can switch it off at any time in Settings. Deleted after 30 days.
Crash reports If the game hits an unexpected error, it sends the error message and technical trace, the game version, the platform, the screen you were on and your player ID, so we can fix the bug. Deleted after 90 days.
Player reports If you report another player in an online room, we record both player IDs, the reason you chose and a note about the race. Deleted after 90 days.
Connection data Your IP address is used to connect you to our servers. To limit abuse we briefly keep a one-way hash of it. Our server's access logs for the game's online service record IP address, the address requested, time and app or browser details. The hash expires after about a minute. Access logs rotate automatically and are overwritten as they fill.

On your device

The game stores your save file (with a backup copy), a queue of results waiting to upload, and any screenshots you take, in the game's own storage on your device. Deleting the game deletes them.

LAN races

LAN races connect devices on the same local network directly. The host's local IP address is shown on screen so others can join. Your callsign, chosen squads, ready state, live race updates and final result are shared with the other devices in the room. This traffic doesn't pass through our servers.

This website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking, and we don't keep visitor logs for it. Its fonts are served by Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google, which receives your IP address and browser details when the page loads. See Google's privacy policy.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal data or share it for advertising. We share it only as follows:

  • Other players see your callsign, scores and race results in online and LAN features.
  • Apple and Google confirm purchases made through their stores. The store connects the purchase to you through a one-way fingerprint of your player ID, never the ID itself.
  • Stripe processes purchases made outside the mobile stores, such as on desktop, and receives your player ID so the purchase reaches the right profile. You enter payment details on Stripe's own page. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Hosting and database providers run the servers and database that store the data above for us, under contracts that only allow them to process it on our instructions.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or to protect players, the game or the public from fraud or harm.

Our providers may process data outside your country. When the law requires it, we use recognised safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

Legal bases

Where laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on:

  • contract, to provide the game, online features and purchases you ask for;
  • consent, for gameplay analytics, which you can withdraw in Settings at any time;
  • legitimate interests, for crash reports, security, fair play and abuse prevention;
  • legal obligation, for keeping purchase and tax records.

Your choices and rights

  • Analytics: switch gameplay analytics on or off in Settings.
  • Callsign: change it in Settings at any time.
  • Access, correction, copy, deletion and objection: depending on where you live, you can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, get a copy, delete it, or object to how we use it.

To make a request, email {{support_email}} with your player ID. You'll find it in the game's Settings screen, shown as "Profile: CVY-…". Because the game has no password, we may ask for details only someone with your device would know before we act, so nobody else can delete or read your data. We reply within 30 days.

Resetting your campaign in Settings starts a new player ID on your device but doesn't delete data linked to the old one. To remove that, send us the old ID before you reset, or email us. See how to delete your data.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to your local data protection authority. We'd appreciate the chance to fix it first.

Children

Crownroad isn't directed at children under 13, or the minimum age for consenting to online services in your country if that is higher. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children under that age. If you believe a child has used our online features, contact us and we will delete their data.

Security

Data moves between the game and our servers over encrypted HTTPS connections. Session tokens and store receipts are stored only as one-way hashes, and access to our servers and database is restricted. No system is perfectly secure. If you find a vulnerability, please report it as described on the support page.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this policy when the game or our practices change and revise the effective date above. For significant changes we'll tell you in the game or on this website before they take effect.
