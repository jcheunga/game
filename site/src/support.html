Purchases

I paid but didn't get my item

The game checks every purchase with our server before adding it, so a slow or dropped connection can delay it. Connect to the internet and restart the game. It checks unfinished purchases again when it starts.

If it still hasn't arrived after a few minutes, email us with your player ID and the order number from your store receipt.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds go through the store you paid with. For the App Store, use reportaproblem.apple.com. For Google Play, follow Google's refund steps. For desktop purchases made through Stripe, email us.

Progress and devices

Can I move my progress to a new device?

Yes. Sign in under Settings → Account with Game Center, Google Play Games, Apple, Google or email, and your progress follows you to the new device. Progress also backs up to the cloud after each won stage. See the Terms of Service for details.

What does "Reset campaign" do?

It clears your campaign and starts a new player ID on this device. Data linked to your old ID stays on our servers until you ask us to delete it, so note your old ID first if you want it removed.

Online play

Challenges or online rooms won't connect

Online features need an internet connection. The rest of the game works offline, and finished results upload the next time you're connected.

How do LAN races work?

Everyone must be on the same local network. The host starts a room and shares the local address shown on their screen, then the others type it in to join. Some public and office networks block device-to-device connections. If joining fails, try a home network or a phone hotspot.

How do I change the name other players see?

Change your caravan callsign in Settings. It can use letters, numbers, spaces, hyphens and underscores, up to 18 characters.

Delete your data

You can delete your account, and everything linked to it on our servers, from the game: open Settings, choose Account, then Delete account. This removes your profile, callsign, sign-ins, results, cloud save, crash reports and analytics straight away, and revokes Sign in with Apple. Your device then starts again as a new guest.

  • We keep only the purchase and refund records the law requires, under a random code that isn't linked to you.
  • If you can't open the game, email {{support_email}} with the subject "Delete my data" and your player ID (in the game under Settings → Account → Player ID). We may ask you to confirm a detail from the game on your device, so nobody else can delete your data. We delete it within 30 days and email you when it's done.
  • Deleting the game from your device removes the data stored on the device.

The Privacy Policy lists exactly what deleting removes and keeps.

Report a security issue

If you find a vulnerability in the game, its servers or this website, email {{support_email}} with "Security" in the subject and enough detail for us to reproduce it. We'll confirm we got it and keep you updated while we fix it.

Please don't access or change other players' data, degrade the service for others, or share the issue publicly until it's fixed. We won't take action against good-faith research that follows these rules.
